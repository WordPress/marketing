# WPTV to YT Publication Process

New content from [WordPress.tv](https://wordpress.tv/), which appears in its [RSS feed](https://wordpress.tv/feed/), is automatically published to [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/wordpress) via [Zapier](https://zapier.com/). This content includes videos at their original quality, thumbnails, titles, descriptions, and tags.

- All videos are posted with the Creative Commons license.
- Due to Google API limits, one video is posted roughly every 15 minutes. The total delay will depend on how many videos were uploaded recently.
- To avoid double-posting, do *not* upload directly to YT. Simply upload to WPTV and let the Zap push it to YT.
- Flagship event videos are excluded from this process and are instead manually edited and posted.
- All YouTube uploads automatically include a line in their description linking to the original content on WPTV.
