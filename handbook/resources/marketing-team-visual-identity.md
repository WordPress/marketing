# Marketing Team Visual Identity

For the WordPress brand visual identity and guidelines, visit the [WordPress Design Library](https://www.figma.com/file/e4tLacmlPuZV47l7901FEs/WordPress-Design-Library?node-id=1%3A2) in Figma for the most up-to-date information.
