# WordPress Showcase

The [Showcase](https://wordpress.org/showcase/) shows the world what can be done with WordPress. It demonstrates the capabilities of the publishing platform with a vast array of use cases, including sites for personal bloggers, small businesses, enterprise companies, and national governments.

- **Primary success metric:** Showcase views
- **Other metrics:** New Showcase submissions, New WordPress installs, WordPress brand sentiment

## Contributing

This is an excellent opportunity to get involved in a way that helps shape how others, especially those who aren’t familiar with WordPress, might come to view the work of brands utilizing WordPress. As the Make Marketing Team updates the content of the Showcase, the team should aim to elevate and maintain the integrity of the platform.

Whether you would like to practice showcase moderation, share your copywriting skills, or capture images from some of the most beautiful sites on the web, there is something to contribute. Here is how Showcase submissions come to the Showcase:

1. New Showcase submissions are automatically checked to ensure they are running WordPress. If they pass automatic review, a [Github issue](https://github.com/WordPress/Marketing-Team/labels/showcase) is created with an AI-suggested rating and other information.
2. Contributors write the Showcase entry description and share it in the [GitHub issue](https://github.com/WordPress/Marketing-Team/labels/showcase).
3. Contributors add the necessary screenshots to the [GitHub issue](https://github.com/WordPress/Marketing-Team/labels/showcase).
4. Showcase admins publish the content to the Showcase.

### Moderating submissions

New Showcase submissions are automatically checked to ensure they are running WordPress. Everything that passes the automatic check has a [GitHub issue](https://github.com/WordPress/Marketing-Team/issues?q=is%3Aissue%20state%3Aopen%20label%3Ashowcase) created for it. Sites will then need to be reviewed based on the guidelines included in the [submission form](https://wordpress.org/showcase/submit-a-wordpress-site/). Blogs and websites powered by WordPress that make it into the Showcase are typically doing one or more of the following:

- Using WordPress in a unique or innovative way.
- Representing a notable organization, government entity, or corporation as an official blog or website.
- Using modern WordPress.

Please keep in mind that sites must be using WordPress and following basic web standards. Common reasons to reject sites include: site SSL is invalid, site contains low-resolution images, site contains excessive ads, and broken styling or links. It is normal for nearly half of all submissions to be rejected for these reasons.

### Writing entries

Showcase submissions needing copy are labeled as [“needs copy” in GitHub](https://github.com/WordPress/Marketing-Team/issues?q=is%3Aissue+is%3Aopen+label%3A%22needs+copy%22). Before you begin, familiarize yourself with the [WordPress Brand Writing Guide](https://make.wordpress.org/marketing/handbook/resources/style-guide-and-brand-book/). Simple Showcase entries are organized in the following way:

- 1st Paragraph: A few sentences about the company, organization, or person that the site is for/about. If there’s an interesting quote to pull directly from the website, feel free to use it.
- 2nd Paragraph: Briefly describe the website, how WordPress has helped it come to life, and what’s unique or interesting about it.

Example Showcase entries:

- <https://wordpress.org/showcase/9to5mac/>
- <https://wordpress.org/showcase/daybreaker/>
- <https://wordpress.org/showcase/thisisfinland/>
- <https://wordpress.org/showcase/hodge-bank/>

Consider using AI to help get started. Example prompt:

> I would like you to act as a copywriter for the WordPress brand. Your tone is casual, respectful, and positive-neutral. Your voice can be characterized as friendly, clear, composed, inclusive, charming, and empowering. Your language style should be clear and straightforward, with a dash of playfulness without being too grandiose, and needs to be understood by people whose first spoken language may not be English.
>
> The WordPress software is flexible, customizable, extensible, reliable, innovative, and scalable for websites of any size.
>
> Please write a website description for **INSERT WEBSITE URL** that aims to show the world what can be done with WordPress and help demonstrate the capabilities of WordPress as a publishing platform with a vast array of use cases of sites from personal bloggers and small businesses to enterprise companies and national governments.
>
> The content should include two paragraphs without headings: first paragraph should be around three sentences and describe the company, organization, brand, or person the website is about; second paragraph should be three sentences or less about how WordPress is being used in an interesting and unique way.

Showcase admins will review all content before publishing it. They may give feedback or ask for edits to ensure consistency in formatting, tone, style across all Showcase entries, etc.
