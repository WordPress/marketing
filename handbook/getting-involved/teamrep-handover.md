# Team Reps

The [list](https://make.wordpress.org/marketing/handbook/about/#current-marketing-team-reps) of the current Marketing Team Reps.

## What is a Marketing Team Rep?

The collaboration efforts across WordPress.org are divided into teams known as the Make WordPress, MakeWP, or Community Teams. For each of the teams, [team representatives](https://make.wordpress.org/updates/team-reps/) can be nominated to facilitate team management, encourage others, and ensure the administrative tasks of the team are completed. Read more in the post on [what it means to be a Team Rep](https://make.wordpress.org/updates/team-reps/). Summarizing the definition from this article:

> “Team Rep is a leadership role that is mostly administrative in nature; it is not a Lead role. Letting go of the Team Rep title is not a loss of status, just a handing off of responsibilities. Someone who is a leader in a team can lead whether they are doing the team rep job or not.”
>
> Essentially, this role involves co-ordinating or driving projects. A list of some of responsibilities included in this role are below.
>
> - Are responsible for communicating on behalf of their team to other contributor teams and sharing this information back with their own team.
> - Help represent the team internally, collaborate with other teams, support contributions from others in the team. Answer requests to create, review or edit copy for other teams.
> - Be part of the interface with the other Make WordPress Teams and leadership.
> - Help onboard new contributors, support returning and existing contributors.
> - Facilitate the planning process for team meetings on GitHub and run the weekly meetings with input from other contributors.
> - Contribute to the agenda and notes, encourage contributors to volunteer for note-taking and the coffee breaks, maintain the schedules and flow.
> - Encourage contributors and address any issues together with others as needed.
> - Update the diary post and team calendar.
> - Contribute to updating the team’s GitHub, and with others who also give time to support this area, its maintenance.
> - Assist with updates to the handbook and updating documentation.
> - Aggregate ideas for new tools and features to help the team.
> - Contribute to contributor events and help plan the team’s involvement.
> - Monitor and answer queries on the Marketing blog make.wordpress.org/marketing comments, along with other contributors
> - Moderate disputes, should they occur, and seek assistance from the Community team where necessary.
> - Encourage team participation and growth in the WordPress community.
> - Generally keep a watch on the moving parts of the team to represent this at meetings and update the team’s GitHub.
> - Administer access rights to tools including Google Drive, GitHub, Make WordPress Marketing blog site.
> - Build on the efforts and contributions of previous team reps and contributors in general.

## How does the team appoint team reps?

Invitations to stand and elections will be held in November each year for team rep roles across the project, to start a one year term from January 1. If you are interested in standing in a future year, do reach out to one of the current representatives.<br>
<br>
Since 2022, Make teams have moved to an annual system, where all contributors can be nominated for a new year, including those who had stood or been nominated before, irrespective of whether they had opted not to stand in previous years. The Marketing Team moved to an annual model during 2020 and 2021, and as part of this transition had a hybrid system in place where new reps were appointed, based on suggestions invited from the team as a whole, and a consideration of their contribution efforts within the WordPress.org Marketing Team. Recommendations could be made throughout the year and these were shared in the team. When a vacancy arose, if willing to stand, those contributors were subsequently appointed.

Team reps are elected for the Marketing Team annually. In the new cycle, the team reps begin their term in the first or second meeting of 2024.

### To do’s for outgoing team reps

The steps below can be done as part of the onboarding and handover for team reps for the new term.

#### Request access rights on the Marketing Team Blog

- Ask in the #Meta channel to add the new team reps as Editors to the Marketing Team Blog.
- Show them around if needed.

#### Request @here Slack channel rights in the Marketing Team Channel

- Raise a Meta ticket or ask in the #Meta channel for @here rights for the new team reps in the Marketing team’s Slack channel (#marketing).
- Ask to remove rights for the previous team reps if they no longer need these.

#### Add admin rights to the marketing contributor badge groups

The two Marketing badges shown on user profiles are controlled by membership in these two groups. Invite the new team reps to request access:

- [Marketing Contributor Group](https://profiles.wordpress.org/associations/marketing-contributor/) – People who have delivered to the work of the marketing team for an equivalent of 10 hours get rewarded with a Global WordPress Marketing Team Contributor Badge.
- [Marketing Team Member Group](https://profiles.wordpress.org/associations/marketing-team/members/) – People who consistently deliver to the work of the marketing over an extended period of time get rewarded with the Global WordPress Marketing Team Member badge.

Then, admit them to the groups and promote them to administrators

### To do’s for incoming team reps

#### Confirm the Marketing Team meeting time

Sometimes team reps need to hold the team meeting at a new time to accommodate their time zones and schedules.

- Confer with fellow team reps to confirm the existing time, or to identify a new meeting time.

#### Editor rights on the Marketing Team Blog

- Change the role of the incumbent team reps to what matches their new roles within the team
