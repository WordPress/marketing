# What are our tasks?

## Marketing Team Tasks

As a team our goal is to provide content and strategy for other Make WordPress teams. To that end, our tasks vary quite a bit from writing copy for events like #WPTranslationDay, to newsletter copy for the Community Team, to ways to promote local Meetups, and more.

When you join the Marketing Team, you’ll never be bored.

## The make.WordPress.org Marketing Team:

- Meets in the [WordPress Slac](https://make.wordpress.org/chat/)k #marketing [channel weekly](https://make.wordpress.org/marketing/handbook/#when-does-the-global-marketing-team-meet).
- Contributes copy to any other WordPress team that requests it.
- Provides marketing strategy for specific projects upon request.
- Overall SEO, including meta descriptions, for WordPress.org pages.
- Oversees and updates the content of [The Showcase](https://wordpress.org/showcase/).
- Works with the WordPress.org Growth Council on initiatives to better promote the project globally.
- Gives presentations at WordCamps and Meetups.
- And much, much more.

Join us on Slack to get started today!
