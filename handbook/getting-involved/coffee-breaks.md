# Coffee Breaks

Coffee breaks are bi-weekly socials held by the Make Marketing team for all WordPress Marketing contributors, and occasional guests. Stop by and meet some of the friendly faces marketing WordPress.

These casual get-togethers can be hosted by anyone, in either [Zoom](https://www.zoom.com/) or [Google Mee](https://meet.google.com/)t. We do ask that everyone abide by the usual [code of conduct](https://make.wordpress.org/community/handbook/wordcamp-organizer/planning-details/code-of-conduct/).

Follow this Google [Calendar with meetings, collaborations, and social activities](https://calendar.google.com/calendar/b/2?cid=NDQyN2g1cnZrN2EwYXA5Y3VpNDI5OGYzY3NAZ3JvdXAuY2FsZW5kYXIuZ29vZ2xlLmNvbQ). We also list important dates in this [Forthcoming Diary Dates](https://make.wordpress.org/marketing/2021/01/13/forthcoming-diary-dates/) post.

The current time for the coffee breaks is 18:30 UTC. We use UTC times in Slack and GitHub to make it easier for contributors to check their local time.

## Facilitating a coffee break

Every coffee break has a facilitator who opens the Google Meet or Zoom, welcomes newcomers, and reminds attendees of the code of conduct. If you would like to facilitate a coffee break, these are the steps to complete:

1. Comment with your interest and availability during the [weekly Marketing meeting in Slack](https://wordpress.slack.com/archives/C0GKJ7TFA) or directly in the [GitHub card for coffee breaks (#23)](https://github.com/WordPress/marketing/issues/23).
2. Once confirmed, add the date and time to your calendar. If you can not make the session, please post on GitHub and on the Marketing Slack and tag the Marketing Team reps to help them find an alternative.
3. When the time comes, open a new [Google Meet](https://meet.google.com/) or [Zoom](https://www.zoom.com/) room (please disable any recording features). Photographs and recordings should not be taken during the coffee break. This is an informal social opportunity to help keep the community connected.
4. Post to [#Marketing channel in Slack](https://wordpress.slack.com/archives/C0GKJ7TFA), letting everyone know the room is available. Here is an example:<br>
    `👋 Good day, everyone. The time has come, and the coffee break is open: [GOOGLE MEET OR ZOOM LINK]`
5. Keep the session open for the 40-minute scheduled time, or up to an hour.
6. Post to [#marketing channel in Slack](https://wordpress.slack.com/archives/C0GKJ7TFA), closing the room. Below is an example:<br>
    <code>This bi-weekly social is held by the Make Marketing team and occasional guests. Stop by and meet some of the friendly faces marketing WordPress. You can volunteer to help facilitate these get-togethers on the <a href="https://github.com/WordPress/marketing/issues/23">GitHub card #23</a> 👋😁☕</code>

## Conversation starters

Conversation usually unfolds naturally, but if you would like to kickstart things, here are a few conversation starters:

- When did you first discover WordPress?
- What’s your favorite dance move?
- What have you read/watched lately?
- Have you travelled anywhere recently?
- If you could instantly learn any language, which would you choose?
- What is your most-used emoji?
- What is your favorite strange fact?
